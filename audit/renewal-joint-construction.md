# Renewal, overlap, and literal-cell bridge (candidate)

The archive uses `u(n)=max(parent(n)+u(n/p), u(n-1), old(n), seed(n))` for n>0, not a sum of four independent cover bounds. `SmoothRenewal.vertices_partition` identifies the first operand with the **disjoint vertex union** of the old-prime smooth block and p times the new-prime smooth block at n/p. Restrict any cover of the whole block to each part; each restriction covers its induced edges, and the cardinalities add because the vertex sets are disjoint. Cross-edges need not disappear. The other three operands are bounds on the whole block (the n−1 bound is extended by inclusion), so they combine with this sum by max, never by addition. This valid max construction does not imply that separately certified overlapping edge packings may be summed.

Define the actual gain d(n) = u(n) − (parent(n)+u(n/p)); the max guarantees nonnegative subtraction and base+d=u(n). Thus max produces the additional amount d relative to the already disjoint recursive decomposition, even when the seed achieving max overlaps it. The gain is not itself a separately disjoint edge packing. Iterate this *numeric equality*, not a putative disjoint decomposition of the seed graph. For cutoff X, let U be the computed max table for n≤X and U(n)=parent(n)+U(n/p) for n>X. For n>0 the target literal identity is

`U(n)=parent(n)+U(n/p)+(if n≤X then sum(c in level.cells, if c.a≤n<c.b then c.height else 0) else 0)`.

`lean/ArchiveRenewalJointCandidate.lean` proposes this identity jointly with `Demand Edge (vertices (insert p Q) n) (U n)`. Inputs are prime freshness, **all-argument** parent/old/seed graph demands and a **finite** pointwise comparison between actual literal cells and the difference computed from the max table. The theorem defines U; neither its recurrence nor its graph demand is an input. However `FiniteCorrectionCheck` is exactly the missing equality between literal cell corrections and those computed by the four-way max: it is not checked or discharged for any full real level by this candidate. The all-argument old and seed demands are substantive, unproved certificate premises, not the desired conclusion, and parent demand must be supplied at every argument. The separate `profile_max_update` candidate identifies the constructed function with the four-way max **only within the cutoff**; the continuation is zero-gain and need not satisfy that max. The bounded literal lookup examples test only three points, not a real level check. Neither candidate has been compiled or axiom-gated here. Instantiation requires all literal level checks, certified old and seed bounds, matching profile/table inputs and archive-byte provenance. The Python byte-regeneration tests are not a kernel proof of archive correspondence.

For 20 levels, compose these statements from a valid zero base, prove zero-at-zero for each constructed profile, and unroll the recurrence into a sum over suffix-smooth factors with occurrence multiplicity. Because this sum is **equal to the single proved demand U20(n)**, it is jointly chargeable in every final smooth block; it is not a sum of merely independent overlapping demands. Partition [1,N] by final-rough q, transfer demand to the complement of the independent set, and reindex `(q,suffix factor)` for each literal cell to the earlier-prime rough integer. These disjoint final blocks give the desired global hcover. Existing factor matching and allocation modules isolate parts of this route but do not discharge the actual level checks or assembled global bridge. The suffix factor multiplicity and duplicate cell occurrences must survive reindexing, and endpoints at zero and X+1 need explicit proofs. This is a precise route, not a proof of hcover.
