## Exact target and semantic boundaries

* `Defs.lean:14–17`: triple-free means there do not exist **pairwise distinct** a,b,c in A with rational `1/a = 1/b + 1/c`. At the final maximum, `A ⊆ Icc 1 N` ensures positive denominators; Lean's totalized division by zero therefore causes no issue. Variants permitting repeated denominators are not established by this result.
* `Maximum.lean:14–20`: `f302 N` is the supremum of cardinalities over the finite powerset of `[1,N]` filtered by that triple-free predicate, hence the intended finite maximum. `card_le_f302` needs precisely subset and triple-freeness, both supplied.
* `commonL` is a **fixed natural number**, a maximum of explicitly defined giant cutoffs (`CommonL.lean:83–88`), not a variable cutoff growing with N. `L ≥ 17` is checked. `rho = totient(roughPrimeModulus L)/roughPrimeModulus L` (301 `Analytic/Defs.lean:23–26`), a fixed density, not the number of rough integers up to N. The 327 Mertens lemma has the same ratio and requires `L ≥ 3` (`RoughCount.lean:322–325`), which is discharged. Its proof and imported constants remain upstream trust obligations.
* The formal claim is `∃ N0, ∀ N ≥ N0, (5/8 + 1/(43200 log L)) N ≤ f302 N`. The threshold is existential, not an explicit numerical N0 or a bound for every N. `∀ d < rho/24, ∃ N0(d), ∀ N ≥ N0(d)` cannot be silently exchanged to a uniform threshold in d. The limiting coefficient rho/24 with no `−1` is not shown as an eventual pointwise bound. The stated liminf is a valid real-analysis consequence, but is not a new Lean declaration here; normalization is at positive N and the N=0 term is irrelevant to liminf.

## Proof-obligation audit

1. `LowerNext.lean:9–39` uses the fixed-L eventual budget with `Kh=128000000`, `Kt=oddBudget L` and fixed slopes; it does not introduce a hidden `N`-dependent L. It requests carrier admissibility, decomposition, and `|C| ≥ (1/2+rho/24)N` using imported theorems. Those imported bounds, including their actual upstream proofs and source/olean correspondence, are not independently verified here; the conclusion is conditional on them.
2. `FromErdos301.lean:58–59` defines O as positive odd n with `4n ≤ N`; `:98–109` supplies `|O| ≥ N/8−1`, including N=0 and integer rounding. Decomposition makes C and O disjoint (`:111–124`), padding is triple-free (`:153–276`), and `card_union_of_disjoint` is used before natural-to-real casting. Hence `(1/2+rho/24)N + N/8−1 = (5/8+rho/24)N−1`. I inspected the padding proof's split: carrier head forces both tails into C; odd-quarter head forces both tails even and then into the top band, where distinctness makes their reciprocal sum strictly less than `4/N`. This is a bridge inspection, not a full audit of its 301 relation lemmas.
3. `endpoint` fixes d first, uses positive gap `g=rho/24−d`, chooses natural `K>1/g`, then `N0=max(M,K)`. For N≥N0, `Ng>1`, so the −1 is absorbed. At d=rho/24 the gap is zero, and this argument cannot remove −1. At Ng=1 weak absorption holds exactly; the proof's strict choice K is sufficient, not necessary.
4. `L≥17` implies `log L>0` and `L−1>0`. With imported `1/1800 < mertensLowerConstant` and `mertensLowerConstant/log L ≤ rho`, strictness is preserved after division by log L and 24, giving `deltaLog < rho/24`. Lean also independently checked `deltaLog>0` in the test file. The older displayed increment is `delta=1/(86400(L−1))`; `delta2=1/(43200(L−1))=2 delta`; since `log L < L−1`, `deltaLog>delta2=2 delta`. This is **more than twice the increment above 5/8**, not more than twice the total coefficient or f302. No external strongest-bound comparison has been made.
