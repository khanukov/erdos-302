## Minimal Lean-facing data and proof interface

A compact *data* schema is `structure Cell where a b h : ℕ`; `structure Level where prime : ℕ; cells : Array Cell`; `structure Ledger where cutoff : ℕ; levels : Array Level`. The prime order, X, C, E and number of levels are fixed targets, not trusted certificates; rho and J are derived in `ℚ`, and correction lookup is `Σ c, if c.a ≤ n ∧ n < c.b then c.h else 0`. The exporter JSON additionally carries C/E for crosschecking only. Supports, bank proof nodes, images, extension records, upstream intervals and theorem witnesses are separate inputs: the 4,705 cells alone cannot justify profile soundness. They may be supplied by existing bank modules, but the linking equality must name their exact outputs and exact archive input interpretation.

Required obligations: (i) byte-to-parsed-data correspondence or an explicitly stated trusted translation assumption, including duplicate-key rejection; (ii) full per-node checker soundness and archive-image/extension correspondence; (iii) upstream theorem applies to the precise prime order, reversed intervals and old-profile values (matching CK does not prove pointwise identity); (iv) seed/projection semantic bounds, recurrence and cell-pointwise equality, separately for all 20 levels and all entries; (v) real endpoint-to-integer interval counting and discrepancy budget, disjoint smooth/rough blocks, reindexing, recurrence expansion, plus finite support; (vi) rational and error arithmetic and the exact final theorem with its assumptions and Lean axiom audit. A report-cell replay or Python hash is not Lean verification of any of these.
